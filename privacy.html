Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Information We Collect

StudyLeem collects minimal data to provide our free service:

  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time on site (via Google Analytics)
  • Contact Messages: Name, email, and message when you use our contact form
  • LeemAI inputs: the questions you type, quiz settings and, only if you choose to upload them, photos of questions or papers. These are used only to generate your response
  • Cookies: Google Analytics uses cookies for analytics

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to contact form submissions
  • To improve our study materials and site experience
  • To generate answers, quizzes and paper feedback with LeemAI
  • To analyze site traffic via Google Analytics

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may collect data according to their own privacy policies:

  • Google Analytics — website traffic analysis
  • Google Gemini API — powers LeemAI; your question text and any photo you upload are sent to Google to generate the response
  • Google Firebase (Firestore & Authentication) — database for study materials and contact messages, and admin sign-in
  • Google Drive — PDF file hosting and viewing
  • Vercel — website hosting and analytics

LeemAI, Quizzes and Paper Photos

Photos you upload to the LeemAI paper checker or attach in chat are compressed in your browser and sent only to process your request. StudyLeem does not save your photos on its server, in a database or in file storage, and we do not write them to our logs. They are forwarded to the Google Gemini API to produce the result and then discarded by StudyLeem. Google processes this data under its own terms and privacy policy, so please do not upload photos that show your name, roll number, address or other personal details.

Your quiz history and class/subject choice are stored only in your own browser (local storage) and can be removed by clearing your site data. AI answers and marks can contain mistakes and are not official FBISE results.

Data Security

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. Contact form submissions are stored securely in our database and only accessible to site administrators. We do not require user accounts or store passwords.

Cookies

Our site uses cookies from Google Analytics. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect site functionality.

Children's Privacy

Our site is designed for students. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 beyond what is necessary to respond to a contact form message.

Contact

For privacy concerns, contact us or email support@studyleem.com.
