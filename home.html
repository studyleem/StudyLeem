What is StudyLeem?

StudyLeem is a free study website for students of the Federal Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education (FBISE), Pakistan. It offers free chapter-wise notes, textbooks, exercises and MCQ explanations for Class 9, 10, 11 and 12, an AI tutor called LeemAI, and a study blog – with no registration needed.

  • Free notes, books and moreNotes, books, exercises and MCQs for Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics, Computer Science, English, Urdu, Islamiat, Pakistan Studies and other subjects.
  • LeemAI – AI tutor for FBISEAsk questions in English, Urdu or Roman Urdu, take AI quizzes with score and review, and get a paper checked from a photo. Photos are not stored on StudyLeem servers.
  • Read online or downloadEvery PDF opens inside the page with a built-in viewer, and can be downloaded for free.
  • Study blogWeekly study tips and exam-preparation guides for Federal Board students.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I download FBISE notes for Class 9, 10, 11 and 12?

StudyLeem offers free chapter-wise FBISE notes, textbooks and exercise PDFs for Federal Board Classes 9 to 12. Pick your class and subject, then open the chapter in the built-in PDF viewer.

What is LeemAI?

LeemAI is StudyLeem's AI tutor, personalized for FBISE. It answers questions from the Federal Board syllabus, generates class and subject quizzes with a score and study suggestions, and checks papers from a photo.
