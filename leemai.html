LeemAI – the AI tutor made for FBISE students

LeemAI is an AI-powered study assistant personalized for the Federal Board (FBISE) of Pakistan. Unlike general chatbots, LeemAI is tuned to FBISE Classes 9, 10, 11 and 12: it follows the Federal Board syllabus, uses textbook terminology, marks in FBISE exam style and understands English, Urdu and Roman Urdu.

Frequently asked questions

What is LeemAI?

LeemAI is the AI-powered study assistant of StudyLeem, personalized for the Federal Board (FBISE) syllabus of Classes 9, 10, 11 and 12. It answers questions, creates quizzes and checks handwritten papers.

Is LeemAI free?

Yes. LeemAI, the FBISE notes and the books on StudyLeem are free to use.

How does the LeemAI quiz work?

Choose your class and subject, optionally a chapter, the number of questions and the difficulty. LeemAI generates multiple-choice questions in FBISE style. When you finish you get your score, a review of every answer with explanations, and a personal study plan with suggestions.

How does the AI paper checker work?

Select your class and subject, enter the total marks and upload clear photos of your solved paper. LeemAI reads your answers and gives question-wise marks, mistakes, model answers and tips. The marks are an AI estimate for practice and are not official FBISE marks.

Does StudyLeem store my paper photos?

No. Photos are compressed in your browser and sent only to process your request. StudyLeem does not save them on its server, in a database or in storage. You can also remove them from your device with one tap.

Which classes and subjects does LeemAI support?

Classes 9, 10, 11 and 12 of FBISE, including Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics, Computer Science, English, Urdu, Islamiat, Pakistan Studies, Economics and Statistics.

Can I ask LeemAI in Urdu or Roman Urdu?

Yes. You can write in English, Urdu or Roman Urdu and LeemAI replies in the same language. You can also attach a photo of a question.
